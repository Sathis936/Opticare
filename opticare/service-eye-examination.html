What Does an Eye Exam Involve?

A comprehensive eye examination is much more than checking how clearly you see. It is a full review of your eye health that can detect silent conditions before they affect your vision.

Your visit takes around 40 minutes and includes a detailed conversation about your lifestyle, vision habits and any symptoms, followed by a series of painless tests on our modern diagnostic equipment.

Digital Retinal Imaging

High-resolution photos of the retina to detect early signs of disease.

Computerised Refraction

Objective measurement of your exact prescription needs.

Glaucoma Screening

Intraocular pressure and optic nerve assessment.

Field of Vision Test

Check your peripheral vision for blind spots.

What's Included

  • Medical & lifestyle history review
  • Visual acuity & refraction testing
  • Digital retinal photography
  • Glaucoma and cataract screening
  • Printed prescription & written report
