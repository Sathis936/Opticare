10 Simple Ways to Reduce Digital Eye Strain

Screen time is up for everyone. Here is how to protect your eyes with the 20-20-20 rule and more.

Eye Health Aug 12, 2026 Dr. Meera Nair 5 min read

10 Simple Ways to Reduce Digital Eye Strain

Table of Contents
  • What Is Digital Eye Strain?
  • 10 Evidence-Based Tips
  • When to Consult an Optometrist

Most of us now spend eight or more hours a day staring at screens — laptops, phones, tablets and televisions. It is no surprise that "computer vision syndrome", also known as digital eye strain, is one of the most common complaints we hear at OptiCare.

What Is Digital Eye Strain?

Digital eye strain happens when your eyes work harder than normal because of prolonged screen use. Symptoms include sore, tired or dry eyes, blurred vision, headaches and neck or shoulder pain.

The good news? It is almost always preventable with a few simple habits.

"Your eyes were built for the horizon, not for a 14-inch screen. Small breaks make a big difference." — Dr. Meera Nair

10 Evidence-Based Tips

1. Follow the 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, take a 20-second break and focus on something at least 20 feet away to relax eye muscles.

2. Optimize Ambient Lighting

Avoid excessive overhead glare. Match your room's ambient lighting with your screen's brightness.

3. Keep an Arm's Length Distance

Position your computer monitor roughly 20 to 28 inches from your eyes, slightly below eye level.

4. Consciously Blink More

People blink 50% less when looking at digital devices. Keep tears flowing with frequent, complete blinks.

5. Use Preservative-Free Lubricating Drops

Artificial tears keep corneal surfaces lubricated and soothe gritty, irritated eyes during long shifts.

6. Increase Font Size & Display Contrast

Make text large enough to read comfortably without leaning forward or squinting.

7. Consider Anti-Reflective & Blue Light Lenses

High-index lenses with multi-layer anti-reflective coatings significantly cut down screen glare.

8. Take Micro-Breaks Every Hour

Stand up, stretch, hydrate, and look out the window to break continuous near-distance accommodation.

9. Check Your Prescription Regularly

Even a minor undetected astigmatism or prescription shift can cause intense headaches during computer work.

10. Schedule an Annual Comprehensive Eye Exam

Regular check-ups ensure early detection of dry eye syndrome, ocular fatigue, and binocular vision changes.

When to Consult an Optometrist

If you experience persistent headaches, double vision, or chronic dry eyes that drops cannot relieve, it is time for a professional assessment. Book your eye test online today for comprehensive retinal and visual analysis.
