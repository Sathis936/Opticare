How Vision Testing Works

Our computerised vision testing uses advanced digital instruments to measure your eyes with exceptional precision. The process is quick, painless and provides your optometrist with exact data to determine the right prescription for you.

Whether you need glasses for the first time or your prescription has changed, our tests cover visual acuity, contrast sensitivity, colour vision and binocular alignment to give you a complete picture of how your eyes perform.

Auto-Refraction

Digital measurement of your refractive error for an accurate starting prescription.

Visual Acuity

Standard and advanced charts to test near, intermediate and distance vision.

Colour Vision Test

Screening for colour deficiency using Ishihara plates and digital tools.

Contrast Sensitivity

Measures how well you distinguish objects from their background in varying light.

What's Included

  • Computerised auto-refraction
  • Visual acuity at multiple distances
  • Colour vision screening
