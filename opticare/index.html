Why Regular Eye Tests Matter

Regular eye examinations help detect vision problems early and protect your long-term eye health.

  • Early detection

    Spot glaucoma, cataract and macular issues before symptoms appear.

  • Accurate prescription

    Up-to-date prescriptions prevent headaches and eye strain.

  • Better eye health

    Personalised advice on diet, screen time and eye-care habits.
