Lens Hygiene

Keep Your Lenses Happy

Simple habits that protect your lenses — and your eyes.

01

Wash Your Hands

Always wash and dry your hands before touching lenses.

02

Use Fresh Solution

Never reuse solution or top it up in the case.

03

Respect Schedules

Replace lenses on time and never sleep in lenses.

04

Clean The Case

Replace your lens case every 3 months.
