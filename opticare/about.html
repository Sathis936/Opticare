Our Story

Caring for Eyes Since 2016

OptiCare began with a simple belief — everyone deserves access to world-class eye care and eyewear that truly fits their life.

What started as a neighbourhood optical store has grown into a full-service eye care centre. Today our certified optometrists, optical specialists and friendly staff serve thousands of families with the same warmth we had on day one.
