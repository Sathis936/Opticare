Comprehensive Dry Eye Management

Chronic dry eye is more than a mild irritation. Left unmanaged, tear film instability causes corneal friction, blurry fluctuating vision, and inflammation. At OptiCare, our specialized dry eye assessment identifies the root cause of your symptoms.

We evaluate both evaporative dry eye (caused by Meibomian Gland Dysfunction) and aqueous-deficient dry eye, prescribing a targeted regimen of clinical treatments and home therapies.

Tear Film Osmolarity

Microscopic diagnostic evaluation of tear salt concentration and evaporation rate.

Meibomian Gland Imaging

Infrared meibography to assess eyelid gland structure and lipid secretion.

Thermal Pulsation Therapy

Targeted gentle heat to melt blocked oils and restore natural tear lipids.

Custom Treatment Plan

Prescription eye drops, punctal plug advice, and nutritional guidance.

What Is Included in the Clinic Visit

  • Non-invasive Tear Break-Up Time (NIBUT) test
  • Fluorescein corneal staining analysis
  • Meibomian gland expression and infrared scan
  • Screen ergonomics and workplace lighting consultation
  • Tailored home therapy kit and product recommendations
